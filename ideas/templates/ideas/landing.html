Track every idea, from spark to shelf

IdeaFlow is a private tracker for the work efforts that could become projects, businesses, or side projects — one place to capture an idea, rank it, and watch it move as it matures.

{% include "ideas/_google_signin.html" %}

Three tabs, one lifecycle

Current

The efforts actively being worked on right now.

Tracking

Everything on the radar, with state and ranking at a glance.

Archive

Shelved or finished efforts, kept for reference.

What each idea holds

A title, summary, and notes; a category and stage with their own color-coded pills; a 1–5 interest rating; a manual rank within its tab; a list of linked resources; and a running log of research entries — topic, focus, context, when it happened, the AI model used, effort and quality ratings, and tokens spent — so the thinking behind an idea isn't lost.

Access is by invitation

Sign in with Google to request access. An administrator assigns which tabs you can see and manage, and whether you can add new ideas.
