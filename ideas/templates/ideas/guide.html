User guide

Turn a promising thought into focused work

IdeaFlow keeps ideas, decisions, research, and results together from the first spark through active work and, eventually, the archive.

Get started

Capture just enough to make the next decision clear.

  1. Sign in with Google. IdeaFlow is invitation-only. A new account can read public projects immediately; an administrator grants access to private workspace tabs.
  2. Open + New Idea. If you have the Add Ideas or Admin role, start with a title, short summary, category, and next action. New ideas begin in Current.
  3. Add context when it helps. Links, parent ideas, stage, interest, rank, notes, repository, visibility, and automation settings are available under “More details and settings.”
  4. Save, then keep the next action current. A useful next action is concrete enough that you—or an agent—can pick up the work without reconstructing the plan.

Understand the lifecycle

Status determines where an idea lives; stage describes how far it has progressed.

Current

Work receiving active attention now. Use this as a deliberately short focus list.

→
Tracking

Work worth monitoring or developing, with filters, rank, stage, next action, and attention signals at a glance.

→
Archive

Finished or shelved work retained with its history, research, resources, and artifacts.

Status and stage are different. Move an idea between Current, Tracking, and Archive to change its workspace. Change its stage—such as Spark, Exploring, Building, Launched, or Stalled—to describe maturity.

3

Work from the idea page

The detail page is the durable record for an effort.

Plan the work

Set an active next action and queue follow-ups. Reorder the queue or mark the active item complete as work advances.

Record research

Use Log research to preserve findings, effort, quality, model, token usage, and open questions. Answer highlighted questions to feed human context into later work.

Keep useful outputs

Add resources for source links and artifacts for reports, files, lists, and other deliverables. Artifacts can also be referenced by other ideas.

Organize related work

Give an idea a parent or create child ideas. Use children when a promising direction deserves its own next actions and history.

A simple weekly rhythm

  1. Review Current and keep only genuinely active work there.
  2. Open Tracking, filter for human input, paused work, or missing next actions, and resolve the important signals.
  3. Update rank, stage, and next action; move completed or dormant work to Archive.
  4. Check weekly summaries and artifacts for portfolio-level outcomes when your role provides access.
4

Use the wider workspace

These tools appear only when your assigned role allows them.

Public projects
The home page shows projects shared with every signed-in user. Public access is read-only unless you also hold the idea’s workspace role.
Tracking filters
Search by text, category, stage, attention, or owner. Sort for human input, family, rank, interest, or update time; edit key fields directly in the list.
Artifacts
Browse reports, summaries, files, and external deliverables across all ideas you are allowed to see.
Feeds
Review incoming articles tied to ideas, read summaries, and rate their usefulness and relevance.
Knowledge Graph
Explore parents and explicit relationships between ideas. Authorized users can review suggested relationships and open Graph Lab.
Persona council and repeat tasks
Optional automation can review stalled ideas or collect recurring results. Configure these on an idea; pause them whenever human direction is needed.
Podcasts
Turn a repeat task into a podcast: set up a show on the idea page, then connect a research idea as its source (add a Supports relationship pointing at the podcast idea). Each due run drafts an episode from that research for you to review, edit, and publish — or unpublish or delete later. Requires the Podcast role.

Know your access

IdeaFlow shows only the controls and workspaces available to you.

RoleWhat it allows
CurrentView and manage ideas in Current.
TrackingView and manage ideas in Tracking.
ArchiveView and manage archived ideas.
Add IdeasCreate ideas; creation does not automatically grant access to every workspace.
Knowledge Graph, Feeds, Weekly SummaryOpen the corresponding specialist workspace.
PodcastSet up a show, connect research sources, and review, publish, or delete episodes — separate from the tab role that lets you edit the idea itself.
AdminFull access, user roles, ownership, and configuration.

If a tab or button described here is missing, your account probably does not have that role. Ask an IdeaFlow administrator to review your access. Being able to read a public project does not grant permission to edit it.
